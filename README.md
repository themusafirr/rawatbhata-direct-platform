## 🌟 Capabilities & Offerings

- 🍔 **Food & Beverage Delivery:** Rapid turnaround ordering from top local diners and restaurants.
- 🛒 **Grocery & Daily Essentials:** Fresh perishables, kirana goods, and pantry supplies.
- 💊 **24/7 Urgent Pharmacy Dispatch:** Emergency medicine pickup and doorstep delivery.
- ⛽ **Emergency Fuel & Mechanical Assistance:** On-road support for vehicles across Rawatbhata township.
- 🔧 **Local Home Services & Handyman:** Verified plumbers, electricians, and technicians.
